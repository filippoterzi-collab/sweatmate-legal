why i’m building sweatmate

august 2026 · filippo

i love cycling, hiking and running — but i kept doing it alone. my friends aren’t into it, and turning up to a structured club felt like too much. i just wanted to find people near me who wanted to go out this weekend.

so i started building a map of sports outings: you see what’s happening near you, tap “i’m going”, sort it out in a group chat, and meet at the pin. and it pulls together the local running clubs and races that today live in a dozen scattered whatsapp groups.

we’re starting in bergamo. if that sounds like something you’d use, join the beta.

❤️ hi, i’m filippo. i love cycling, hiking and running — but i kept doing it alone. my friends aren’t into it, and showing up to a structured club felt like too much.

i wanted a simple way to find people near me who wanted to go out this weekend — and to gather all the local running clubs and races in one place, on a map. so i started building sweatmate.

— filippo, bergamo 🫶

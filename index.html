find people to do sports with, near you

the map of sports outings around you. runs, rides, hikes. you see one, you say “i’m going”, you meet at the pin.

be the first to know when we launch. no spam, ever.

here’s what your weekend could look like

not an algorithm, not a feed. just a normal saturday, with people who wanted the same thing.

☀️

saturday morning

open the map. see who’s going out near you today.

👀

right after

tap “i’m going” on a run, or propose your own in two taps.

💬

before you go

sort out the details in the group chat: time, pace, meeting point.

📍

you meet

you all show up at the pin. no awkward, just people who wanted the same thing.

🙌

next weekend

you’ve got people to move with. add the ones you keep meeting.

❤️ hi, i’m filippo. i love cycling, hiking and running, but i kept doing it alone. my friends aren’t into it, and showing up to a structured club felt like too much.

i wanted a simple way to find people near me who wanted to go out this weekend, and to gather all the local running clubs and races in one place, on a map. so i started building sweatmate.

filippo, bergamo 🫶

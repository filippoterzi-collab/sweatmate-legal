community guidelines

sweatmate is people meeting in the real world to do sport together. these rules keep it safe and welcoming. by using the app you agree to them.

be real

  • one account, your real name and a real photo of your face.
  • you must be 18 or older.
  • post activities you actually intend to do; show up if you say “i’m going”.

be kind

  • no harassment, hate speech, threats or discrimination of any kind.
  • no spam, scams, or promoting unrelated products in chats.
  • this is not a dating app — the match is on the activity, not the person. unwanted advances aren’t welcome.

be safe

  • meet in public places, tell someone where you’re going, use your judgment.
  • assess the route, the weather and your own ability before any outing — you take part at your own risk.
  • never share someone else’s private information.

no dangerous or illegal content

  • nothing illegal, dangerous, sexual, or that puts others at risk.

reporting & blocking

every profile, activity and chat message has report and block. we review reports and remove content or accounts that break these rules. blocking someone hides you from each other everywhere in the app.

questions or something to report? write to filippoterzi@aiutomazione.com.

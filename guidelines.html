community guidelines

sweatmate is people meeting in real life to do sport together. these rules keep it good.

by using the app you agree to these guidelines. break them and we can remove your content or your account.

be real

  • one account, your real name, a real photo of your face.
  • you must be 18 or older.
  • post outings you actually intend to do, and show up if you say “i’m going”.

be kind

  • no harassment, hate speech, threats or discrimination of any kind.
  • no spam or scams, and no pushing unrelated products in the chats.
  • this is not a dating app. the match is on the activity, not the person. unwanted advances aren’t welcome.

be safe

  • meet in public places, tell someone where you’re going, use your head.
  • check the route, the weather and your own level before any outing. you take part at your own risk.
  • never share someone else’s private information.

nothing dangerous or illegal

  • no illegal, dangerous or sexual content, and nothing that puts other people at risk.

reporting and blocking

every profile, activity and chat message has a report and a block button. we read the reports and take down content or accounts that break these rules. when you block someone, you two stop seeing each other everywhere in the app.

something to report, or a question? write to hello@sweatmate.health.
